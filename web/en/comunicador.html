A communication board for everyone, no account needed

The child taps the pictures, builds a sentence, and the phone, tablet or computer says it out loud. It is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) board with a small starter vocabulary: core words, feelings, needs, people, places, actions and play.

  • Private: everything happens on this device. Nothing is sent to Puente or to any AI, there is no tracking and no account is needed.
  • Works offline: after you open it once with internet, the board is saved and works without a connection.
  • The voice is the device's own. If it has no English voice, the sentence is still shown in writing.
  • Tip: use it in full screen or add it to the home screen so it does not close by accident.
